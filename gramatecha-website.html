Our Story

Isang Platform Para sa Bawat Mag-aaral

GRAMATECHA was built with one goal in mind: to make learning Pasulat at Pasalitang Komunikasyon accessible, clear, and relevant for every Filipino student.

Language is the foundation of everything - how you express your ideas, how you write your thoughts, how you connect with other people. And yet, so many students struggle not because they lack the ability, but because the available resources are hard to follow.

We built GRAMATECHA to change that.

🇵🇭

Para sa mga Pilipinong Estudyante

Modern. Libre. Batay sa KWF.

🏛️ Our Alignment with the KWF

All content on GRAMATECHA is based on the official language standards set by the Komisyon sa Wikang Filipino (KWF) - the national government agency responsible for developing, preserving, and promoting the Filipino language.

  • Terminolohiya na nakakaisa sa opisyal na pamantayan
  • Mga halimbawa na nakaangkop sa modernong paggamit ng Filipino
  • Wastong gramatika ayon sa KWF na mga alituntunin

❓ Help Center

Mga Madalas na Tanong

Got questions? Here are the answers to the ones we hear most often.

🖥️ About the Platform

Libre ba ang GRAMATECHA? ▼
Yes - completely free. All reading materials and video lessons on GRAMATECHA are available at no cost. No registration, no subscription, no hidden fees.
Kailangan ko bang mag-sign up para magamit ang platform? ▼
Hindi na. You can browse and access all lessons without creating an account. Just go to the Lessons page and start learning.
Anong devices ang maaaring gamitin para sa GRAMATECHA? ▼
GRAMATECHA works on any device - desktop, laptop, tablet, or mobile phone. As long as you have a browser and internet connection, you're good to go.
Anong wika ang ginagamit sa mga aralin? ▼
Most lessons are written and recorded in Filipino, with some concepts explained using a mix of Filipino and English for clarity. This is intentional - so that the content is accessible to more students.

📖 About the Content

Sino ang gumawa ng mga aralin sa GRAMATECHA? ▼
Our lessons are developed by educators with backgrounds in Filipino language, communication, and teaching. All content is reviewed against the official guidelines of the Komisyon sa Wikang Filipino (KWF).
Tama ba ang mga leksyon dito ayon sa opisyal na pamantayan? ▼
Yes. All content on GRAMATECHA aligns with KWF standards - the official national language authority of the Philippines. We regularly update our materials to reflect any changes in official language guidelines.
Para sa anong level ng pag-aaral ang GRAMATECHA? ▼
GRAMATECHA is designed primarily for Senior High School and College students, but the content is structured so that anyone - regardless of level - can follow along. Lessons are tagged: Panimula (Beginner), Intermediate, and Advanced.
Mayroon bang activities o assessments? ▼
Some lessons include guided reflection questions and practice activities built into the reading material. We are continuously adding more interactive content to the platform.

🎬 About Video Lessons

Saan naka-host ang mga video lessons? ▼
All video lessons are streamed directly on the platform through an embedded video player - no need to be redirected to another site.
May mga subtitle o closed captions ba ang mga videos? ▼
Yes. Most video lessons include Filipino subtitles for accessibility. We are working to add closed captions to all video content.
Maaari ko bang i-download ang mga aralin? ▼
At this time, lessons are only available for online viewing and reading. We are exploring offline access options for future updates.

🔧 Technical & Support

Hindi gumagana ang isang aralin. Ano ang dapat kong gawin? ▼
Try refreshing your browser or clearing your cache first. If the issue continues, please contact us through the Contact page and we'll look into it right away.
Maaari ko bang mag-suggest ng bagong paksa? ▼
Absolutely. We welcome suggestions from students and teachers. Use the Contact page to send us your idea - we read every message.
May certificate ba ang GRAMATECHA? ▼
We are currently working on a certificate feature for students who complete full units. Stay tuned for updates.

Hindi mo pa nakita ang sagot sa iyong tanong?

Send us a message and we'll get back to you within 1–2 business days.
